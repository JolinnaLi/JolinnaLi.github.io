home remedies | 10 min

Experimental film shot with iphone during Yeyin Residency in Taichung, Taiwan December 2025 - January 2026

Lost things in a dark river, a hand combing through it. A child on the search for order does it in secret to preserve its sacredness. Time precipitates. It gathers in low places. But the juvenile rituals remain. Praying morphs into home remedies made with glistening wax apples before finally taking form through the iris of the camera, where another system of faith is constructed.

the womb is an altar of all the things i miss | Gallery Show

January 2026 Lei Gallery in Taichung, Taiwan
in collaboration with Autumn Samone
Do you have any secret dreams, leaping like moving feet? We invite you into a cradle for all the desires that have not gone away, only transferred and displaced; things that have been given and kept for good.

This Duo Show, as a part of the Yeyin Art Residency, consists multi-media installation, painting, and three films:
  • home remedies, by Jolinna Li
  • Ballooning under a dirty cocoon, by Autumn Samone
  • Rebel Without a Cause, by Judy Zhao, writing by Jolinna Li

Interactive part of the exhibit, as an excercise in ritual making, a modern altar was made with code and analog buttons.

Participants can hear a previous message recorded by another person and then proceed to record their own voice message, with the new message overwriting the previous one.

This was made with the help of James Huang.

the hand is no one's | Trio Show by JAR

April 2025 Ruffin Gallery in Charlottesville, Virginia in collaboration with Autumn Samone and Rian Gonzalez

Stories can be thought of as arrows, or bullets, a bowed line from exposition to rising action to climax to resolution. But sometimes the line begins to curl into itself, spiraling inwards and outwards.

JAR is an entity of three artists, a collective vehicle that then creates a space to hold an imagined future that is reborn from past entanglements. The gallery is reconfigured as both an archive of memory and a space of community formation, where collective histories give rise to new modes of encounter.

Jolinna Li is an artist, writer, and filmmaker born in Charlottesville, Virginia; growing up between the United States, Taiwan, and China. Li is currently an Aunspaugh Fellow at the University of Virginia for the 2026-2027 academic year.

In her work, she is interested in both personal and collective myth-making, as individuals and communities negotiate their relationship to the camera and the frame. Her practice considers how images construct belief, and how memory, performance, and narrative become entangled through acts of filming and viewing.

Li is the recipient of the 2025-2026 VMFA graduate arts fellowship award and the 2024 UVA Distinguished Artist Award. Her 2024 feature film Gui Zhou was selected at the Asian American International Film Festival in New York, NY. They received their BA from the University of Virginia in Studio Art and English in 2025, concentrating in film and creative writing.
